What I do

I work in construction project management and operations at Northland Precast and Rush Steel Fabrication & Design, the businesses my family built. I'm also launching Axis Metals, a structural steel fabrication company in Toronto.

Early in my degree I was set on finance. I joined the York Investment Club and interned at a venture capital fund in Dubai. Then I chose to come home and work in the family business instead.

The Shelburne project

My family's business is developing a new precast plant in Shelburne, Ontario. Mattbuilds is where I share the progress from that site, from the first foundations to the finished plant, and what I learn along the way.

Why this site exists

When I was deciding whether to join the business, nobody was showing what that path actually looks like: the early mornings, the mistakes, earning respect from people who've been doing this for decades. So I'm writing it down as I go.

Every week I share one honest lesson from the shop floor, the setbacks, and working alongside family. It's written for anyone a few steps behind me who's weighing the same decision.

What I believe

How you do anything is how you do everything. Opportunities don't happen, we create them, by showing up every day in work, family and faith, and giving your best even when nobody's watching.

I come from a family of seven, and outside work I volunteer with my church's youth group.
