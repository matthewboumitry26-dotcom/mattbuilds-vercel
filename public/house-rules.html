House rules

How I run this site and my social accounts, and what to expect in the comments.

What's welcome

  • Questions, disagreement and your own experiences in a family business.
  • Criticism of my ideas, as long as it's about the idea and not the person.

What gets removed

  • Personal attacks, harassment, hate or threats.
  • Spam, self-promotion and unrelated links.
  • Anyone's private information, including names of clients or employees.

What I won't share

I write about real work, so I protect the people in it. I don't name clients, share pricing or project details, or post private matters about my family or the people I work with.

Corrections

If I get something wrong, I fix it in the post and add a note at the bottom saying what changed and when. I don't quietly delete mistakes. If you spot one, tell me.

Opinions

Everything here is my own view, not the official position of Northland Precast, Rush Steel, Axis Metals or Schulich.
