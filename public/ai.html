How I use AI

The stories on this site are mine. Here's exactly where AI helps and where it doesn't.

Where I use AI

  • Planning: brainstorming post ideas and outlines from my own notes.
  • Editing: checking my drafts for clarity, spelling and anything that breaks my style rules.
  • Repurposing: turning a post into a draft caption, LinkedIn post or weekly email, which I then edit.
  • Building this site: I used ChatGPT (OpenAI) to redesign and develop the current Mattbuilds website, including its layout and features. The first prototype was created with Claude (Anthropic).

Where I don't

  • I don't use AI to invent experiences, quotes, numbers or people.
  • I don't use AI-generated photos of me, my work or job sites.
  • AI never publishes, replies to comments or messages, or has my logins.

How I check

I check every fact, figure and source against the original before it goes live. If I can't confirm something, it doesn't get published.

Who is accountable

Me. If an AI tool helped and something is wrong, that's still my mistake to own and fix. See my house rules for how I handle corrections.
